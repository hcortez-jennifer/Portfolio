Célesse

Year: 2026

Category: Skin care

Services: Brand identity / visual identity / web design

Who is Célesse

Célesse is a premium winter-focused skincare brand dedicated to protect, hydrate, and restore your skin during the cold months. Rooted in the belief that winter should never compromise one's skin health or confidence, Célesse use powerful natural ingredients that shields against harsh cold air and winds leaving your skin soft, glowing, and well-protected.

Design goals

The goal was to create a brand that feels warm, calm, and gentle while keeping it elevated and confident.

  1. Avoid overly clinical feeling by creating an identity that feels warm, comforting, and emotionally connected across packaging, digital, and social applications.
  2. Use soft neutrals, elegant typography, and spacious layouts to reinforce the brand’s quiet yet confident aesthetic.
  3. Illustrate winter-flowers to visually represent resilience, softness, and renewal.
  4. Optimized for all devices to ensure responsiveness.

Colors

The color palette was chosen to reflect winter warmth and softness. Inspired by quiet winter mornings, natural light, and comfort, the palette combines soft neutrals with cool muted tones to create a visual identity that feels calm, elegant, and reassurance.

  1. Primary Color (#F7EFDA) – Winter Ivory: Reflects warmth and softness. Used as the main background color.
  2. Secondary Color (#517493) – Frosted Blue: Used for text to contrast with the primary color.
  3. Neutral Color (#362722) – Taupe: A warm neutral is used to elevated the brand. Used as a secondary background color.
  4. Accent Color (#56061D) – Mulbery: Adds a sense of richness and confidence. Used for buttons, small components, and decorative design elements to help stand out.
